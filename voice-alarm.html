How it works / limits

• Auto-pilot (ON by default): record or upload → transcribe → AI-organized title + bullets → countdown starts by itself — no time mentioned means alarm in 3 seconds. One tap on the 🚀 chip switches to manual review.
• Cloud engine (recommended): paste a free Gemini API key (aistudio.google.com/apikey — 2 clicks, no card) and everything happens in one ~2-5 second call: transcription, cleanup, organizing, and time extraction. Excellent with Arabic & mixed audio. Local engine: Whisper inside your browser — private and offline, but CPU-slow. Cloud failures auto-fall back to Local.
• ⭐ Small model is the quality pick for Arabic / mixed Arabic-English audio (≈250 MB, downloaded once, then cached & offline). Auto-detect lets Whisper pick the language per recording; force English or Arabic if detection guesses wrong.
• After transcription the app auto-analyzes: first sentence → title, the rest → bullet points (fillers removed, duplicates merged). Everything stays editable.
• Fuzzy times understood: “a couple of minutes” = 2m, “a few minutes” = 3m, “some minutes” = 10m, “in a bit / soon” = 5m, “later” = 1h, “half an hour” = 30m, «بعد شوية» = 5m, «نص ساعة» = 30m, «كم دقيقة» = 10m, «ثواني» = 10s.
• No time mentioned → alarm after 3 seconds showing your organized notes. Snooze = 5 seconds (testing mode).
• 🔁 Spaced repeat: on ring, “Next” re-queues at 5m → 30m → 2h → 1d → 3d.
• Ringtones start soft and escalate. The alarm needs this tab to stay open. Everything is stored locally in your browser.
